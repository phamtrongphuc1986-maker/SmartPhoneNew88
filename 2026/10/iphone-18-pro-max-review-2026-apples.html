Cameras

The camera system is where the iPhone 18 Pro Max makes its biggest statement. The rear setup features three 48MP sensors: a main camera with variable aperture ranging from f/1.48 to f/4.0 and sensor-shift optical image stabilization, a 48MP periscope telephoto with 4x optical zoom, and a 48MP ultrawide, backed by LiDAR.

The variable aperture on the main camera is the headline feature. In low light, the lens opens to f/1.48 to drink in light, producing night shots with remarkable detail and controlled noise. In bright scenes, it stops down to f/4.0 for sharper depth of field across the frame. This flexibility is something photographers have wanted on phones for years, and Apple has implemented it elegantly.

The 4x periscope telephoto is excellent for portraits and distant subjects, retaining texture at a level that used to require dedicated camera gear. The ultrawide, now also 48MP, finally matches the main camera's sharpness, making landscape and architecture shots consistently great.

The front camera jumps to 18MP with Center Stage and autofocus. Selfies are sharper and better exposed, and Center Stage keeps you framed during video calls even when you move around.
