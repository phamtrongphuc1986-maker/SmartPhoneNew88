Vivo V80 Review: A Camera-First Mid-Ranger With Serious Battery Ambitions

Vivo's V series has quietly become one of the most reliable picks in the upper mid-range, and the new Vivo V80 looks like its strongest entry yet. Launched in India on October 6, 2026, with sales beginning October 13, 2026, the V80 brings ZEISS optics to a friendlier price point, pairs them with a huge 7,200mAh battery, and wraps it all in a slim, durable body. Design & Display

The Vivo V80 measures 157.52 x 74.33 x 7.69 mm and weighs 203 grams â€” slim and comfortable for a phone packing this much battery. It comes in three colors: Sunrise Anthem, Horizon Blue, and Stellar Black. Build quality gets a meaningful upgrade this generation with IP68 and IP69 ratings, meaning the phone is protected against dust, submersion, and even high-pressure water jets. That level of protection is still uncommon in this price bracket and is a genuine differentiator.

The display is a 6.59-inch AMOLED panel with a 1.5K resolution of 2750 x 1260, a smooth 144Hz refresh rate, and HDR10+ support. Brightness is a highlight: 5,000 nits of local peak brightness and 1,800 nits in high-brightness mode, which should keep the screen legible outdoors. For streaming, gaming, and everyday scrolling, this is a display that punches above its class.

Performance

Under the hood sits the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, built on a 4nm process. It is paired with 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM and your choice of 128GB, 256GB, or 512GB of UFS 3.1 storage. This is a balanced mid-range chipset: efficient for daily tasks, smooth for the 144Hz display, and capable enough for popular mobile games at sensible settings.
